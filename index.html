HISN (حِصْن) is an Islamic-centered parental control application built for Muslim families in the United States. This Privacy Policy explains how HISN handles your information. We built this app to protect children, not to collect data. Please read this carefully — it is written in plain language, not legal jargon.

1. Who We Are

HISN is an independent application developed by a member of the American Muslim community. It is not a corporation. It is not funded by venture capital. It is not designed to profit from your data. The app exists solely to help Muslim parents protect their children from harmful digital content.

2. What Data HISN Collects

The short version: HISN stores everything locally on your device. We do not have servers that store your personal information. We do not sell data. We do not share data with third parties. We do not run ads.

Data stored locally on your device:

Data What it is Why we need it
Parent PIN A 4-digit PIN you create To lock parent settings so children cannot change them
Blocked apps list Package names of apps you chose to block To know which apps to block
Scheduled apps list Package names of apps blocked during scheduled times To apply time-based blocking rules
Schedule settings Prayer times, bedtime, school hours you configured To know when to activate scheduled blocking

Data we do NOT collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Your child's name, age, or any information about them
  • Which apps your child tried to open or was blocked from
  • Your location beyond the city you manually select
  • Usage statistics, analytics, or behavioral data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprints
  • Any content from your child's messages, photos, or files

3. How HISN Works — Accessibility Service

Important disclosure about Accessibility Service: HISN uses Android's Accessibility Service to monitor which app is currently open on the device. This is required to block apps in real time. We are required by Google Play policy to disclose this clearly.

HISN's Accessibility Service does the following:

  • Detects when a blocked app is opened and immediately closes it
  • Monitors the device screen to prevent access to HISN's settings in Android without the parent PIN
  • Checks the current time against your schedule to apply time-based blocking

HISN's Accessibility Service does NOT do the following:

  • Read, record, or transmit any text you type
  • Access your messages, emails, or any content you write
  • Take screenshots or record your screen
  • Monitor your browsing history or search queries
  • Send any data off your device

All Accessibility Service processing happens entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

4. Device Administrator Permission

HISN requests Device Administrator permission to prevent the app from being uninstalled by a child. This permission:

  • Prevents the app from being deleted without the parent PIN
  • Does NOT allow HISN to remotely wipe your device
  • Does NOT allow HISN to enforce password policies on your device
  • Can be revoked at any time by a parent through HISN's Settings screen using their PIN

5. Prayer Times — External API

HISN calculates Salah times using the Aladhan API (aladhan.com). When the Schedule screen loads, the app sends your selected city's coordinates (latitude and longitude) to Aladhan's servers to retrieve prayer times for the current date.

  • Only the city coordinates you selected are sent — not your real GPS location
  • No personal information is included in this request
  • Aladhan is a free Islamic service — you can review their privacy policy at aladhan.com

6. Children's Privacy

HISN is a tool for parents to protect children. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. The app is installed and configured by parents. Children do not create accounts, enter personal information, or interact with any data collection.

HISN complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

7. Data Security

All data HISN stores lives on your device in Android's secure app storage (SharedPreferences and AsyncStorage). This data is:

  • Not accessible to other apps on your device
  • Deleted automatically when HISN is uninstalled
  • Never transmitted to any server operated by HISN
